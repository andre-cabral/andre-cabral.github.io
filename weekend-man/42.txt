THE END GAME

<h2>
PLAYING TO WIN
</h2>

<h3>
END OF EACH ROUND
</h3>

1. Check if any obiectives have been met and assion obiective points
2. Issue one objective point for each enemy Warrior each player has taken out.

Repeat for each round until all rounds have been played. The number of rounds will be
specified in the Skirmish Rules.

<h3>
END OF THE GAME
</h3>

The game ends if any of these conditions have been met; whichever comes first:

1. It's the end of the final round
2. A specific Winning Objective (WO) is achieved
3. All Warriors on one team are taken out

<h3>
WINNING CONDITIONS
</h3>

In point games, the Objective Points are totalled up at the end of the battle, and the
winner is announced. Even if all Warriors on one side are taken out, the winner is still
decided on Objective Points (unless the Skirmish rules specify otherwise).

Some games will have one winning condition called a Winning Objective (WO). If a
WO is achieved at any point during the battle, then the player who achieved it is the
winner, and the game ends immediately. Objective points will not serve a purpose in
these games.

<h3>
PRIZES
</h3>

Prizes can be Tokens, free rolls on the Searched Gear Table to use in the next battle or a
specific item or gear of your choosing. The prize(s) will be stated in the Skirmish Rules
so everyone knows what they are playing for.
