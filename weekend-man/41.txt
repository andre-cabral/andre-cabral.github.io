ATTACK RULES

<h2>
LINE OF SIGHT & COVER
</h2>

<h3>
LINE OF SIGHT
</h3>

Draw an imavinaru line from the ton of the attacking model'e head to anu part of the
target model you want to attack - but not including the base. If you can see any part of
that target model, they are in the line of sight, and that Warrior can attack them.

If it is not obvious that the target model is in the line of sight, crouch down to table level
and look from directly behind the head of the attacking model towards the target.

<POSTIT>
Models can look and attack in any direction
regardless of where they are facing.
</POSTIT>

<h3>
COVER MODIFIERS
</h3>

If 50% or more of a Warrior is hidden by terrain, obstacles or another Warrior, they are
In Cover. If you disagree with your opponent about the %%, then you can roll-off.

When a target is In Cover, they are harder to hit. Represent this by applying a -1
modifier to the Attack Characteristic of the attacking Warrior.

For example, if a Warrior's Attack Characteristic is 3, they attack with 3 dice. If the
target is hidden by 50% or more, the -1 modifier is applied. This modifier reduces the
attack roll now to 2 dice.

<POSTIT>
Terrain plays a huge part in Weekend Warriors, so
use that cover wisely. Of course, if you'd rather
charge right at your enemy, then that's good fun too;
but beware of the consequences!
</POSTIT>
