ATTACK RULES

<h2>
TAKING DAMAGE
</h2>

<h3>
DAMAGE
</h3>

Every Warrior has a health limit represented by 10 Hearts. Each time a Warrior receives
a point of Damage, 1 Heart is lost. If they receive multiple points of Damage in one
attack, multiple hearts are lost.

When all hearts are lost, the Warrior can no longer continue the fight. They
removed from the battle immediately. They've just taken too many injuries to continue,
but don't worry, they can fight in future battles.

Warriors removed from the battlefield take all their gear with them.

<POSTIT>
Taking a Healer or medi packs with you into battle
helps to keep your Warriors in the fight for longer.
Spend your Warrior Tokens wisely, though, as medi
packs come at a cost!
</POSTIT>
