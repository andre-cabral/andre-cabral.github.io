ATTACK RULES

<h2>
ATTACK ACTION SEQUENCE
</h2>

<h3>
ATTACKING -1-3 ACTIONS
</h3>

Attacking costs 1-5 Actions depending on the weapon used. Lach weapon will come
with an Action Characteristic that will tell you how many Actions it will take to use it.
The more complex the weapon or the more skill needed to operate it, the more Actions
it will take. A sniper rifle, for example, will use 3 Actions while a simple Dagger will use
1 Action. The Attack Sequence is the same for ranged and close combat weapons.

<h3>
THE ATTACK SEQUENCE
</h3>

1. First, designate the enemy Warrior as the target you want to attack and tell the other
player which weapon you will be attacking with.
2. Deduct the number of Actions required to use the weapon.
3. Roll the number of dice in your chosen weapons Attack Characteristic.
    • Any rolls of 1 are counted as automatic epic fails and no modifiers can be
applied. However, some rules and special weapons will allow 1's to be re-rolled.
    • Any rolls of 6 are counted as critical hits and automatic wounds. The target
cannot roll for an Armour Save and receives the number of Damage as stated
in the attacking Warrior's weapon characteristics. Take the number of Damage
for each critical hit.
4. For any other result, add the modifier from the attacker's Power Characteristic.
5. Compare the modified result with the target's Reaction characteristic. If the attacker
equals or exceeds that Reaction value, they hit them with a normal hit.
6. Standard hits can be defended against using an Armour Save. At this stage in the
sequence, move on to Armour Saves. You can find Armour Saves on the next page.

CRITICAL HITS = AUTOMATIC WOUNDS
