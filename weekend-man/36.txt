ACTION RULES

<h2>
OVERWATCH
</h2>

<h3>
OVERWATCH - 2 ACTIONS
</h3>

You can use 2 Actions to put a Warrior into Overwatch. Think of this as taking guard
over a line of fire.

To go into Overwatch, use 2 Actions and designate the weapon your Warrior will use.

When in Overwatch, any enemy Warriors who move into the line of sight of the
Warrior in Overwatch - and who are within the range of the designated weapon -
receive 1 attack. However, an Overwatch attack only allows 1 dice to be rolled for that
weapon, regardless of its Attack characteristic.

<h4>
Continuous Movement:
</h4>

Overwatch applies to each continuous movement action. This means that if an enemy
Warrior carries out 2 Moves in line of sight as one continuous movement action, the
Warrior in Overwatch can only make 1 attack against them.

<h4>
Normal Movement:
</h4>

If an enemy Warrior makes a Move into the Overwatch Warrior's line of sight, then
carries out an Attack, then makes another Move that is still in the line of sight, the
Warrior in Overwatch can make 2 attacks against them; one attack for each movement.

<h4>
Cover:
</h4>

Cover is ignored when in Overwatch, but all other normal attacking rules apply - except
you may only roll the 1 attack dice.

<POSTIT>
Overwatch is a handy way to potentially inflict
minor damage on your enemy and make them think
twice about breaking cover. If you feel this might
be too complicated for younger players, then
exclude it from the game.
</POSTIT>
