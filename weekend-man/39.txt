<h3>
ARMOUR SAVES
</h3>

The Warrior's Reaction helps them defend against attacks and represents their ability to
absorb hits, block or move out of the way. If a critical hit was achieved, it was such
great shot that it found a weakness in the defences and caused automatic damage. No
Armour Saves can be made against critical hits.

If a normal hit was achieved, however, the bullet, arrow, fist or sword hit its target, and
now the target Warrior must rely on their armour to prevent damage.

<h3>
NORMAL HIT SAVES
</h3>

For each normal hit, the target rolls a dice. The target must equal or beat the value
shown on their Armour Characteristic.
