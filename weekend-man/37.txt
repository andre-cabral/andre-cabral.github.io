ACTION RULES

<h2>
SEARCHING
</h2>

<h3>
SEARCHING -1 ACTION
</h3>

Skirmishes can include crates and chests that your Warriors can search for useful items
You can represent these crates and chests with toys, paper drawings, pieces from other
games or have fun crafting your own. My kids love the search rule! You can add crates
or chests to any of your skirmishes. I recommend using 1-4 that are evenly spaced across
the battlefield.

<h4>
Searching:
</h4>

To search, use 1 Action and then roll 1D6.

• If the result equals or beats the Warriors Reaction Characteristic, then they fail to
find anything.
• If the result is less than the Warriors Reaction Characteristic, then they find
something!

<h4>
If Successful:
</h4>

If successful, you can roll once on the Searched Item Table towards the back of the
book (or from additional tables as they are released) and add that item or condition to
the Warrior Card of the Warrior who carried out the search.

Following a successful Search, remove the crate or chest from the battlefield.

<h4>
If Unsuccessful:
</h4>

If your Warrior fails to find anything, nothing happens, and they carry on
with their activation. They may use another Action if they have any left
to carry out another Search.

<POSTIT>
The higher the Reaction Characteristic, the better.
As the Armour dictates Reaction, the idea here is
that the lighter Armour allows a Warrior to be more
nimble in their search.
</POSTIT>
